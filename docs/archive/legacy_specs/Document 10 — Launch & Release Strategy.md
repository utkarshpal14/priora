# Document 10 — Launch & Release Strategy

## 1. Product Positioning

### What Priora Is

Priora is a productivity platform that helps users manage tasks, deadlines, reminders, goals, schedules, and unfinished work in one place.

### What Priora Is Not

- AI assistant
- Chatbot
- Exam planner
- Placement tracker
- Team collaboration platform
## 2. Target Users

### Primary Users

- College students
- University students
- Competitive exam aspirants
- Freelancers
- Solo professionals
